## Scope

Synthetic renders are easier than real photographs; these numbers bound the pipeline, not field recognition rates. The simulated probe stands in for the lab endpoint; no live measurement was made. Verification outcomes in this run are `VerifiedCompleteSimulated` by construction and are never labelled live.


## Findings worth stating plainly

- **Over-confidence on degraded images.** On the dark, blurred and glare renders (`a02`, `a03`, `a05`, `f04`, `r04`) Muse still marked the cable near end as *Observed*. Those scenes counted as "flagged uncertainty" only because the post-repair item was Unknown. A single-look analyzer would have accepted four of the five fake fixes; the deterministic gate denied all five. This is the reason the gate exists, measured.
- **SAM misses the close-ups.** All three SAM disagreements (`c04`, `f05`, `r02`) are close-up renders where the cable is a large flat yellow shape with the plug filling the port; SAM returned an honest no-match rather than a wrong box. Wide views matched 15 of 15. On real photographs this may invert; the close-up prompt may also need to be "yellow cable plug" rather than "yellow ethernet cable".
- **Wrong-asset label was caught by the model.** On the SW02 scene Muse did not mark the chassis as Observed for LAB-SW-01 (0 wrong-asset claims), but asset identity is bound by the fixture, not by the model, so this is a courtesy signal only.
- **Every verified outcome was `VerifiedCompleteSimulated`.** No live measurement exists in this run and none is claimed.
